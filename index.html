School equipment, organized

Know what equipment your school has—and where it is.

The School Equipment Tracking System helps school personnel view equipment details, locations, categories, conditions, and current status in one easy-to-use place.

Equipment overview

Information available in the system

Equipment records — Items in the equipment list
Categories — Categories represented in the records
Available — Equipment currently available
Other statuses — In use, under maintenance, lost, or damaged

What you can do

Browse the equipment table, filter records by category, sort by name, ID, or category, and search for a record by its name, identification number, or category.

Equipment records are shared through the connected Supabase project.
